<span class="about_title">ARTIST STATEMENT</span>

<p>

<span style="font-style: italic; padding; 0px">“This is what it feels like to be here.”</span>

<p>

I create mixed-media works using collage, original photography, and acrylic paint to explore
my connection to the natural world. Inspired by the diverse southern California landscape, I
transform unique encounters into layered compositions to evoke memory, sensation, and place.
I carefully select mixed-media materials to mirror the variety of perceptions I experience in
my environment.

<p>

Although photography often informs my process, I’m not interested in portraying the world as
it appears. Both the photographs I capture and the images stored in my memory seep into my
consciousness and find their way into my work, for instance in where I place collage pieces
or how I apply and scrape back into paint. I also employ line, shape, color, and texture to
define and highlight specific features of my experiences. My objective with each piece is to
convey a distinct sense of presence, beauty, and the unexpected, with the hope that it resonates
with viewers as well. 

<p>

<div class="about_title">BIO</div>

<p>

Darlene Laguna, a mixed-media artist based in Orange, California, draws inspiration from her
engineering background, which instilled in her a sense of precision, logic, and analytical
thinking. However, it was through hiking, exploring the natural world, and landscape photography
that she discovered a newfound sense of freedom and expansion that led her to her art-making
practice. Primarily self-taught, Laguna’s mixed media work encompasses a diverse range of
techniques, including landscape photography, collage, and acrylic painting. These elements
come together to create layered, abstract compositions that delve into the beauty, presence,
and emotional resonance of nature. Her work has been showcased in numerous juried exhibitions
and galleries.

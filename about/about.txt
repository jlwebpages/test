<span class="about_title">ARTIST STATEMENT</span>

<p>

<span style="font-style: italic; padding; 0px">“This is what it feels like to be here.”</span>

<p>

I create mixed-media works using collage, original photography, and acrylic paint to explore
my connection to the natural world. Inspired by the diverse southern California landscape, I
transform unique encounters into layered compositions to evoke memory, sensation, and place.
I carefully select mixed-media materials to mirror the variety of perceptions I experience in
my environment. I employ line, shape, color, and texture to define and highlight specific
features of my experiences. My objective with each piece is to convey a distinct sense of
presence, beauty, and the unexpected, with the hope that it resonates with viewers as well.

<p>

<div class="about_title">BIO</div>

<p>

Darlene Laguna, a mixed-media artist based in Orange, California, has a sense of precision,
logic, and analytical thinking drawn from her engineering background. Through hiking, exploring
the natural world, and landscape photography, she discovered a sense of freedom and expansion
that led her to art-making. Her mixed media work encompasses a diverse range of techniques,
including photography, collage, and acrylic painting. These elements come together to create
layered, abstract compositions that delve into the beauty, presence, and emotional resonance
of nature. Her work has been showcased in numerous juried exhibitions and galleries.

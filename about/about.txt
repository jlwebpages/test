<span class="about_title">ARTIST STATEMENT</span>

<p>

<span style="font-style: italic; padding; 0px">“This is what it feels like to be here.”</span>

<p>

I create mixed-media works using collage, original photography, and acrylic paint to explore my
connection to the natural world. Inspired by the diverse southern California landscape, I transform
my encounters into layered compositions to reflect on memory, sensation, and place. I carefully
select materials to mirror my perceptions. I employ line, shape, color, and texture to define and
highlight specific insights of my experiences. My objective is to convey a distinct sense of presence,
beauty, and the unexpected, and hope it resonates with those who view my work.

<p>

<div class="about_title">BIO</div>

<p>

Darlene Laguna, a mixed-media artist based in Orange, California, has a sense of precision,
logic, and analytical thinking drawn from her engineering background. Through hiking, exploring
the natural world, and landscape photography, she discovered a sense of freedom and expansion
that led her to art-making. Her work encompasses a diverse range of techniques, including
photography, collage, and acrylic painting. These elements come together to create layered,
abstract compositions that emulate the beauty, presence, and emotional resonance of nature.
Her work has been showcased in numerous juried exhibitions and galleries.

<span class="art_paragraph">

Often, what I remember after a hike and taking a variety of photos, is not one specific image that I want to capture in my work.
Instead, what lingers are pieces of random moments—a patch of color, the texture of tree trunk, or a swath of brush along the
side of a trail, branches and leaves tossing in the wind.

</span>

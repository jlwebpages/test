<span class="art_paragraph">

Surprising horizons are often visible on the side of the freeways here in southern California - the foothills and mountains are never that far away.  As you’re driving you catch glimpses of them out of the corner of your eye, you detect hints of vaster spaces. And if you are driving at night, these horizons can take on a glowing, sometimes ethereal presence.

</span>
